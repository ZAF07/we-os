# Campaign Creation Requirements

## The campaign creation process collects temporary, campaign-specific information required to plan and execute one marketing campaign.

## The campaign workflow must inherit the approved company profile, audience knowledge, positioning, brand rules, compliance rules, and historical evidence created during onboarding.

⸻

# 1. Campaign Request

## Collect:

- Campaign name
- Reason for creating the campaign
- Product, service, feature, event, or initiative being promoted
- Campaign owner
- Relevant stakeholders
- Required approval process

⸻

# 2. Business Objective

## Collect:

- Primary business objective
- Desired commercial outcome
- Priority level
- Why the campaign is needed now
- Business problem the campaign should solve

Example objectives:

- Generate qualified leads
- Increase sales
- Launch a product
- Increase trial registrations
- Improve customer retention
- Build category awareness
- Re-engage inactive customers
- Promote an event

The campaign must have one primary objective. Secondary objectives should not weaken the primary objective.

⸻

# 3. Target Audience

## Collect:

- Primary audience segment
- Secondary audience, if required
- Geographic market
- Language
- Buyer role
- Funnel stage
- Existing or new customers
- Audience exclusions
- Audience-specific problems, motivations, and objections

Where possible, the user should select from approved audience profiles created during onboarding rather than recreate them.

⸻

# 4. Offer and Conversion

## Collect:

- Offer being promoted
- Customer value
- Pricing or promotion
- Eligibility conditions
- Offer validity period
- Primary call to action
- Destination after the call to action
- Required conversion action
- Sales or operational capacity

## Example conversion actions:

- Purchase
- Book a consultation
- Request a quotation
- Register
- Download
- Subscribe
- Start a trial
- Visit a location

⸻

# 5. Campaign Context

## Collect:

- Campaign start date
- Campaign end date
- Important milestones
- Seasonal or market context
- Relevant company events
- Product availability
- Geographic limitations
- Dependencies on other teams or systems
- Known risks or constraints

⸻

# 6. Budget and Resources

## Collect:

- Total campaign budget
- Production budget
- Distribution budget
- Available team members
- Available creators or subject-matter experts
- Existing assets that can be reused
- Required external services
- Publishing capacity

For an organic-only campaign, collect the available time and production capacity instead of treating distribution as free.

⸻

# 7. Channel Preferences

## Collect:

- Required channels
- Preferred channels
- Prohibited channels
- Existing channel audiences
- Channel-specific constraints
- Publishing frequency limitations
- Whether the platform may recommend alternative channels

The final channel selection should be based on the audience, objective, content format, available resources, and existing channel performance.

⸻

# 8. Messaging Inputs

## Collect:

- Main message the client wants communicated
- Product or offer facts
- Key benefits
- Supporting proof
- Required talking points
- Required terminology
- Messages to avoid
- Competitor comparisons, if permitted
- Required disclaimers

The workflow should not treat the client’s proposed message as automatically correct. It should evaluate it against customer evidence, positioning, and the campaign objective.

⸻

# 9. Creative Direction

## Collect:

- Required content formats
- Preferred creative direction
- Reference campaigns or examples
- Existing assets
- Required visual elements
- People, products, or locations that must appear
- Creative styles to avoid
- Accessibility requirements
- Asset production limitations

## Possible formats include:

- Social posts
- Short-form video
- Long-form video
- Articles
- Email sequences
- Landing pages
- Case studies
- Infographics
- Webinars
- Downloadable resources

⸻

# 10. Measurement Requirements

## Collect:

- Primary success metric
- Secondary metrics
- Baseline performance
- Target result
- Measurement period
- Attribution window
- Available analytics systems
- Required tracking events
- Reporting frequency

Metrics must align with the campaign objective.

## Examples:

Objective Primary Metric
Awareness Qualified reach or brand lift
Engagement Meaningful engagement rate
Lead generation Qualified leads
Sales Revenue or completed purchases
Retention Repeat purchase or renewal rate
SEO Qualified organic traffic or conversions
Email Conversion rate or revenue per recipient

Avoid using impressions, likes, or clicks as primary success metrics unless they directly represent the campaign objective.

⸻

# 11. Experimentation Requirements

## Collect:

- Main campaign hypothesis
- Assumptions that need validation
- Variables that may be tested
- Minimum test duration
- Minimum useful sample size
- Conditions for stopping or changing the campaign

## Potential variables include:

- Message
- Offer
- Call to action
- Content format
- Creative concept
- Publishing schedule
- Landing-page structure

⸻

# 12. Compliance and Approval

# Confirm:

- Applicable onboarding compliance rules
- Campaign-specific legal requirements
- Restricted claims
- Required disclaimers
- Platform policies
- Approval stakeholders
- Approval deadlines
- Content that requires manual review

No asset should be published before required approvals are completed.

⸻

↓
Define objective and desired customer action
↓
Provide offer, constraints, budget, and timeline
↓
Import relevant historical performance and evidence
↓
AI diagnoses the campaign situation
↓
AI identifies missing or contradictory information
↓
User resolves critical information gaps
↓
AI proposes campaign strategy
↓
Human reviews and approves strategy
↓
AI creates campaign plan
↓
Human reviews and approves plan
↓
AI generates production briefs and asset requirements
↓
Assets are created and reviewed
↓
Compliance and brand validation
↓
Final human approval
↓
Campaign is scheduled and launched
↓
Performance is monitored
↓
AI recommends optimisations
↓
Human approves material changes
↓
Campaign closes with reporting and captured learnings

Minimum Required Campaign Inputs

A campaign should not proceed without:

- One primary objective
- One primary audience
- One desired customer action
- A defined product or offer
- A clear timeline
- Available resources or budget
- A measurable success metric
- Required approval stakeholders
- Evidence supporting factual or promotional claims

If these inputs are missing, the platform should stop planning and request the specific missing information.

⸻

Campaign Boundary

The campaign workflow should not redefine durable company information unless the campaign reveals that the existing onboarding profile is incorrect or outdated.

New validated knowledge should be proposed as an update to the company profile rather than silently changing persistent company context.

⸻

Agentic AI Control Points

Human approval should be required before:

1. Finalising campaign strategy
2. Finalising the campaign plan
3. Approving factual or promotional claims
4. Publishing assets
5. Making material changes to the offer, budget, audience, or positioning
6. Pausing or terminating the campaign

Routine analysis, drafting, scheduling preparation, monitoring, and reporting can be automated within approved rules.
