# Client Onboarding Requirements

## The onboarding process collects durable company-level information required to generate business-specific marketing outputs.

## Campaign-specific objectives, offers, audiences, budgets, and deadlines must be collected separately through a campaign brief.

⸻

# 1. Business Profile

## Collect:

- Company name
- Company description
- Industry and category
- Products or services offered
- Business model
- Revenue model
- Markets and locations served
- Typical sales or purchasing cycle
- Primary conversion actions
- Website and relevant business links

⸻

# 2. Customer Understanding

## Collect:

- Primary customer segments
- Buyer roles or decision-makers
- Customer problems and pain points
- Desired customer outcomes
- Purchase motivations
- Common objections
- Reasons customers delay or reject a purchase
- Customer language used to describe the problem
- Existing reviews, testimonials, interviews, or sales notes

⸻

# 3. Positioning and Differentiation

## Collect:

- Core value proposition
- Primary customer promise
- Key differentiators
- Main competitors
- Alternative solutions customers may use
- Reasons customers choose the company
- Defensible claims
- Evidence supporting those claims
- Positioning, topics, or comparisons to avoid

⸻

# 4. Brand Identity

## Collect:

- Brand mission
- Brand values
- Brand personality
- Desired customer perception
- Tone of voice
- Writing style
- Approved terminology
- Prohibited terminology
- Messaging guidelines
- Visual identity guidelines
- Logo, colours, typography, and design assets
- Examples of content that represents the brand well
- Examples of content the brand dislikes

⸻

# 5. Marketing Foundation

## Collect:

- Current marketing channels
- Website and landing pages
- Social media accounts
- Email marketing platform
- Existing content library
- Existing campaigns
- Historical campaign performance
- Current customer journey
- Current conversion funnel
- Existing SEO topics and keywords
- Existing calls to action
- Current marketing strengths and weaknesses

⸻

# 6. Legal, Compliance, and Operational Constraints

## Collect:

- Industry regulations
- Advertising restrictions
- Required disclaimers
- Restricted claims
- Prohibited topics
- Geographic restrictions
- Cultural or language requirements
- Internal approval process
- Brand approval requirements
- Legal approval requirements
- Available team capacity
- Content publishing limitations

⸻

# 7. Platform and Data Access

## Request access where applicable:

- Website CMS
- Website analytics
- Google Search Console
- CRM
- Email marketing platform
- Social media platforms
- Social media analytics
- Customer support data
- Sales data
- Asset storage
- Previous campaign reports

Access should be optional during initial onboarding but required before workflows that depend on the relevant platform or data.

⸻

# 8. Supporting Materials

## Allow customers to upload:

- Brand guidelines
- Product documentation
- Sales decks
- Customer research
- Buyer personas
- Competitor research
- Customer reviews
- Testimonials
- Previous campaign briefs
- Previous campaign reports
- Existing marketing content
- Legal and compliance policies
- Frequently asked questions
- Sales and support transcripts

The platform should extract information from these materials and ask the customer to verify the extracted facts.

⸻

⸻

# Onboarding Boundary

## Do not collect the following during company onboarding:

- Campaign objective
- Campaign-specific audience
- Campaign offer
- Campaign budget
- Campaign timeline
- Campaign channels
- Campaign message
- Campaign deliverables
- Campaign success metrics

These belong in the campaign brief created when the customer starts a new campaign.

⸻

# Recommended Onboarding Flow

Create account
↓
Enter basic company information
↓
Connect website and business profiles
↓
Upload existing documents and assets
↓
AI extracts company and brand information (NOT IN V1)
↓
Customer reviews and corrects extracted information
↓
Customer completes missing information
↓
Platform generates onboarding profiles and rules
↓
Customer approves the final company profile
↓
Company context becomes available to all future workflows

## Avoid forcing customers to complete one large questionnaire. Use document ingestion, automated extraction, progressive questions, and explicit customer confirmation.
