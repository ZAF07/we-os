# The Brand DNA is human-authored, from an admin-curated questionnaire

The Brand DNA is authored by the business itself, always. There is deliberately **no "paste your website URL and we'll generate your brand DNA" flow** — that is a gimmick, it is frequently wrong, and grounding a professional marketing system in model-guessed truth hollows out its core premise. Onboarding instead walks the business through a guided questionnaire that asks precisely what is needed to represent their brand and their customers accurately.

The questions are **curated by the platform admin**, not generated by a model. The question set lives in Postgres (versioned, editable without a deploy) and is the single artifact driving three things: the onboarding wizard UI, the shape of the rendered Brand DNA, and what the Stage 0 gate enforces as Required.

The questionnaire asks **only for facts the business owner uniquely knows** — what they sell, price point, who buys, what problems those buyers have, why customers choose them over alternatives, geography, languages, constraints, budget. Crafted artifacts — positioning, value proposition, messaging, brand voice as written, channel selection — are **produced by the engine and approved by the business at the stage gates**. The DNA template already drew this line correctly by asking *"why customers choose them over alternatives"* (a fact the owner knows) rather than *"what is your value proposition"* (an artifact we owe them); the shipped onboarding wizard blurred it, asking for value proposition, customer promise, and differentiators, and the new-campaign wizard asked the user to pick channels.

## Consequences

- Both wizards are rewritten. Beyond removing the crafted-artifact questions, they currently under-collect: four Required DNA fields (price point, geography/service area, languages, budget range) and two of the three Required KPI tiers (Marketing KPI, Creative KPI) are never asked for, so **every business completing onboarding today would fail the Stage 0 gate**.
- Changing the question set changes what the gate requires, so already-onboarded businesses need an "answer N new questions" path rather than a silent retroactive gate failure.
- Shorter onboarding, and the product's value is legible on day one: the business supplies truth, we-OS supplies craft. That division is the whole pitch — it is what replaces a marketing hire or an agency retainer.
